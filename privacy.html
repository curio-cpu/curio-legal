Privacy Policy — Curio

Last updated: September 22, 2026

1. About Curio

Curio is a content creation and publishing service designed to prepare educational and cultural videos and, with the user's authorization, publish them on TikTok.

2. Information we use

When you connect your TikTok account to Curio, we may receive the information made available through TikTok's authorization system, including basic account information required to identify the authorized TikTok account.

Curio may also process video files, scripts, audio, captions and other content provided or generated for publication.

3. How information is used

Information is used to provide Curio's content creation and publishing features, including preparing videos and transmitting authorized content to TikTok for publication at the user's request.

4. TikTok

Curio uses TikTok's developer services and APIs to connect an authorized TikTok account and publish content. Your use of TikTok remains subject to TikTok's own terms and privacy policies.

5. Data sharing

Curio does not sell personal information. Information and content may be transmitted to service providers or platforms when necessary to provide the requested Curio features, including TikTok.

6. Data security

Curio takes reasonable measures to protect information used by the service. Access to connected accounts is based on the permissions authorized by the user.

7. Your rights

You may request information about personal data associated with Curio, request correction or deletion where applicable, or withdraw your authorization for connected services.
